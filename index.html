About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer and Information Sciences at the University of Delaware. My research focuses on wearable sensing, activity recognition, on-device machine learning, and context-aware health interventions.

Research

My work develops smartwatch-based systems that use Apple Watch sensor data to distinguish sitting, standing, and walking in real time. I focus on personalized, on-device models for improving just-in-time adaptive health interventions.

Projects

On-Device Posture Detection

Personalized machine learning framework for distinguishing sitting, idle standing, and walking using smartwatch sensor data.

On-device ML Posture Detection Activity Recognition

Context-Aware Nudging System

Just-in-time adaptive intervention framework that uses contextual signals to improve the timing and relevance of health prompts.

JITAI Decision Trees Health Interventions

Sedentary Behavior Analytics

Data analysis pipeline for studying sitting bouts, walking bouts, nudge outcomes, and behavioral responses from wearable sensor data.

Python Data Analysis Behavior Modeling
