About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer and Information Sciences at the University of Delaware. My research focuses on wearable sensing, activity recognition, on-device machine learning, and context-aware health interventions.

Research

My work develops smartwatch-based systems that use Apple Watch sensor data to distinguish sitting, standing, and walking in real time. I focus on personalized, on-device models for improving just-in-time adaptive health interventions.

Projects

  • Track & Text: Apple Watch-based posture and activity detection system.
  • Context-aware nudging: Personalized prompts to reduce sedentary behavior.
  • On-device ML: Lightweight machine learning for wearable health applications.
