About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the University of Delaware working on wearable sensing, on-device machine learning, and context-aware interventions to reduce sedentary behavior.

Research

My research focuses on distinguishing sitting, standing, and walking using smartwatch data and designing personalized just-in-time adaptive interventions.

Projects
