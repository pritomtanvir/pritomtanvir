About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer and Information Sciences at the University of Delaware. My research focuses on wearable sensing, activity recognition, and context-aware interventions to reduce sedentary behavior using smartwatch data.

Research

My work develops smartwatch-based systems that use sensor data from devices such as the Apple Watch to distinguish sitting, standing, and walking. I focus on personalized, real-time models that support just-in-time health interventions.

Projects

  • Track & Text: Apple Watch-based posture and activity detection system.
  • Context-aware nudging: Personalized prompts to reduce sedentary behavior.
  • On-device ML: Lightweight machine learning for wearable health applications.
